Or paste rows from Excel

Copy the rows straight from the Excel sheet (header row optional). Columns: CUSTOMER NAME · AREA · RATE SCHED · CONTRACT NO. · MONTHS UNPAID · DUE DATE · PAYMENT DATE · ARREARS · LATEST BILL AMOUNT · TOTAL BALANCE AMOUNT (10 columns). The message uses the TOTAL BALANCE AMOUNT as the amount; rows with no balance are skipped.

How counting works: each click on Prep Email or Prep SMS (or on Outlook / Messenger, when they have to generate first) adds 1 to the Generated column on Accounts for every pasted contract number found there. Typing or pasting only previews; it is not counted. Clicking the same thing again for unchanged data is not counted twice.

    Variables you can use
    [NAME] account name
    [CONTRACT] contract no.
    [AMOUNT] amount
    [MONTHS] months unpaid
    [DUEDATE] due date
    [BILLING_MONTH] e.g. June
    [BILLING_MONTH-1] e.g. May
    [BILLING_MONTH+1] e.g. July
    [BILLING_MONTH_YEAR] e.g. June 2026
    [BILLING_MONTH_YEAR-1] e.g. May 2026

    The billing month comes from the due date of the first pasted row. Spacing is applied automatically: one blank line between header, accounts and footer, so extra blank lines in the boxes below do not matter. Name / contract / amount / months / due date are only filled in the per-account loop.
